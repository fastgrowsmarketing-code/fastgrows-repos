## Fonts
- **Social posts:** Atkinson Hyperlegible, for every piece of text on the design (`fonts/atkinson-hyperlegible/`).
- **Blog posts:** Archivo Black (`fonts/archivo-black/`).
- Both fonts are free under the SIL Open Font License (licence files sit beside the fonts).

## Logo
- Files are in `logos/`.
- Never recolour the logo.
- Black logo on light backgrounds, white logo on dark backgrounds.
- Always in the **upper right corner** of social designs, at the same size on every post and slide.

## Every social post must have
- The logo, upper right.
- **www.fastgrows.com centred at the bottom.** Compulsory.
- The same heading size, body text size and logo size as the rest of the series.
- An attractive, realistic background photo (not cartoon or illustration). Stock for this lives in `../backgrounds/`.

## Never on social posts
- Dates (keep posts evergreen).
- Vahi's name.
- Rocket clichés, emoji overload, or anything that looks obviously AI-generated (for example broad underline highlights or templated AI layouts). Everything should look human-made and unique.

## Blog SEO
- Title under 55 characters.
- Meta description under 150 characters.
- Never change an existing post URL when editing its title.
- Cover image 1200x630 (see `../blog/covers/`).

## Writing
Indian English. Fact-check every claim. Never copy content. Ethical marketing focused on long-term growth over shortcuts. Never share or publish content anywhere without Vahi's go-ahead.
