Two posts a week for three weeks and one in week four works well. The posts link to each other, so publish them in this order and update the bracketed link placeholders as each one goes live.

## Before you publish each post

- Replace every placeholder in square brackets, such as [Get in touch with us], with a real link to your contact, service or WhatsApp page.
- Add your own touch: one real client example, opinion or local detail per post makes it clearly yours and stronger for SEO.
- Use a real photo or a Canva design in brand colours (orange, black, white) for the featured image, and add the alt text given.
- Add FAQ schema for the FAQ section (Blogger: paste JSON-LD in HTML view; WordPress: Rank Math or Yoast FAQ block).
- Set the custom permalink (slug) and search description (meta) in Blogger's post settings.
- Post 4 (DPDP Act): recheck the implementation dates on the MeitY website on the day you publish.
- Post 2 (WhatsApp): recheck current WhatsApp Business broadcast limits and API pricing on Meta's site.

## After you publish

- Submit the URL in Google Search Console (URL inspection, request indexing).
- Share a short version on LinkedIn and Instagram, and a link on WhatsApp Status.
- Turn each post into 2 or 3 Reels or carousels (post 5 explains how).
