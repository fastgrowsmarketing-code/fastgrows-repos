Ask a friend how they found their last CA, dentist or coaching class and you will hear a new answer more often now: "I asked ChatGPT." Or they typed a question into Google and read the AI summary at the top without clicking anything.

This is the shift every business owner needs to understand. Search is no longer only ten blue links, and Google is no longer the only place customers search. A growing share of people get a ready-made answer from an AI tool, and that answer names a few brands. If yours is not one of them, you are invisible at the exact moment someone is ready to buy.

The work of getting mentioned in those answers is called generative engine optimisation, or GEO. Some marketers call it answer engine optimisation (AEO). The label matters less than the method, so let us get practical.

What is generative engine optimisation?

Generative engine optimisation is the process of making your business easy for AI systems to find, understand, trust and quote. It covers Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot and Gemini.

Traditional SEO asks, "How do I rank on page one?" GEO asks a different question: "When an AI tool writes an answer about my industry, why would it include me?"

The good news is that GEO is not a separate universe. Most AI search tools pull from the open web and from search indexes. Strong SEO fundamentals are still the foundation. GEO simply raises the bar on clarity, credibility and consistency.

Why this matters for Indian businesses right now

Three things have changed in a short time:

  1. Zero-click searches are rising. When Google shows an AI summary, many users get what they need without visiting any site. Being cited inside that summary is now part of visibility.
  2. Buyers ask longer, conversational questions. People no longer type "CA Vadodara". They ask, "Which CA in Vadodara is good for GST filing for a small trading business?" AI tools handle these questions well, and they reward content that answers them directly.
  3. Trust signals travel further. AI systems cross-check what your website says against reviews, directories, news mentions and social profiles. Mixed signals make you easy to skip.

How AI tools decide which businesses to mention

No AI company publishes a full ranking formula, so be wary of anyone who promises a "guaranteed ChatGPT ranking". What we can observe from testing and from public guidance is that AI answers tend to favour sources that:

  • answer the question clearly and early on the page
  • are consistent about who the business is, what it does and where it operates
  • show real expertise through specifics such as process details, prices, examples and named authors
  • are mentioned by other credible sources, not just by themselves
  • are technically easy to crawl and read

Google has stated that the same SEO best practices apply to its AI features, and that there are no special tricks required. That is useful advice. It means the businesses that do the basics well have a head start.

A practical GEO checklist for business owners

1. Answer real customer questions in plain language

Collect the questions your sales team, receptionist or WhatsApp inbox hears every week. Each important question deserves a clear answer on your website.

Put the direct answer in the first two or three sentences, then expand. For example:

How long does a website SEO audit take?
For a small business website of up to 50 pages, a proper SEO audit usually takes five to seven working days. Larger e-commerce sites take longer because every product template needs checking.

That structure is easy for a human to skim and easy for an AI system to quote.

2. Build clear "about the business" signals

AI tools need to understand your entity: who you are, what you do, where you serve and who stands behind it. Make sure you have:

  • a detailed About page with founder or team names and real experience
  • your name, address and phone number written the same way everywhere
  • an updated Google Business Profile (more on this in our guide on how Google Business Profile can make or break your marketing)
  • consistent descriptions on LinkedIn, Instagram, JustDial, IndiaMART or any directory you use

If your website says "digital marketing agency" and your directory listing says "IT solutions company", you are sending mixed signals.

3. Add structured data

Schema markup tells search engines what each part of a page means. For most small businesses, start with:

  • Organization or LocalBusiness schema on the home or contact page
  • FAQPage schema where you have genuine question-and-answer content
  • Article schema on blog posts, with author details
  • Product or Service schema where relevant

Structured data is not a magic switch, but it removes guesswork for machines reading your site.

4. Show first-hand expertise

AI-generated content is everywhere, and much of it says the same thing. What stands out is information only you can provide:

  • your own process, step by step
  • real price ranges, even if approximate
  • examples from your work (with client permission)
  • mistakes you have seen and how you fixed them
  • local knowledge, such as how buyers in Gujarat behave differently from buyers in Mumbai

This is the "experience" part of Google's E-E-A-T idea (experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trust). It is also what makes a page worth citing.

5. Earn mentions beyond your own website

AI systems look for agreement across sources. A business that is mentioned in a local news story, a trade association list, a podcast, a guest article and genuine customer reviews looks far more credible than one that only praises itself. It is the same reason backlinks matter for website growth.

Start small. Speak at a local business meet-up, contribute expert quotes to industry publications, list yourself in relevant professional directories and ask happy clients for honest reviews.

6. Keep your technical basics clean

None of the above helps if crawlers cannot read your site. Check that:

  • important pages are not blocked in robots.txt
  • the site loads quickly on mobile data, not just office Wi-Fi
  • key content is in HTML text, not hidden inside images or PDFs
  • you have an XML sitemap submitted in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools

Bing matters more than people think, because several AI assistants draw on Bing's index. Its webmaster guidelines are worth a read.

7. Track your AI visibility

There is no perfect tool for this yet, so start manually. Once a month, ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google ten questions your customers would ask. Note whether your business appears, which competitors appear and which sources are cited.

In Google Analytics 4, watch for referral traffic from sources such as chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai and gemini.google.com. The numbers may be small at first, but these visitors often arrive with strong intent.

Common GEO mistakes to avoid

  • Stuffing pages with AI-written filler. Thin content that repeats what everyone else says gives an AI tool no reason to cite you.
  • Chasing hacks. Hidden text, fake reviews and keyword-stuffed FAQ pages can damage trust with search engines and customers alike.
  • Ignoring your Google Business Profile. For local queries, it is often the first source AI answers draw from.
  • Forgetting the human reader. If a page is written only for machines, it will not convert the people who do click.

The bottom line

Generative engine optimisation is not a replacement for SEO. It is SEO done with more honesty and more clarity. Businesses that explain what they do in plain words, prove it with real experience and stay consistent across the web will keep showing up, whether the customer searches on Google or asks an AI assistant.

Start with one step this week: list the ten questions your customers ask most and check whether your website answers each one clearly.

Frequently asked questions

Is generative engine optimisation different from SEO?
GEO builds on SEO. The foundations are the same: quality content, technical health and trust. GEO puts extra focus on clear answers, structured data and mentions across the web, because AI tools summarise and cite sources rather than just listing them.

Can I pay to appear in ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?
You cannot pay to be included in the organic AI answer itself. Some platforms now show ads near AI answers (see our guide to ChatGPT ads in India), but organic inclusion depends on the quality and credibility of your content.

How long does it take to see results from GEO?
If your site already has decent SEO, improvements in clarity and structured data can show results within a few weeks to a few months. Building authority through mentions and reviews takes longer.

Do small local businesses need GEO?
Yes. Local questions such as "best physiotherapist near Alkapuri" are exactly the kind of queries people now ask AI tools. A strong Google Business Profile and clear service pages make a big difference.
